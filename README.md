## Projects

### OpenEx 3.0
**What it is:** A practice cryptocurrency exchange where people can buy and sell pretend coins using pretend money. It was my three week capstone project.

**How it works:** The backend keeps a precise record of every transaction, the same way an accountant records money going out of one account and into another, so the numbers always add up. It also matches buyers with sellers, giving priority to the best price first and the earliest order second. The website shows the live list of buy and sell orders, updating as things happen. I also built an AI assistant called Candle that people can chat to for help with trading questions.

**Built with:** Kotlin, Spring Boot, React, WebSockets, Python, LangChain, Ollama (Groq as a backup), Docker. Hosted on Render and Vercel.

[View on GitHub](https://github.com/sellwane7/openex3.git)

### DeskFlow
**What it is:** An internal IT help desk for a company. When something goes wrong, such as a broken laptop or a login problem, an employee logs a request on the site. The IT team can then see it, work on it and update its progress.

**How it works:** Users have to sign in securely before they can see anything, so people only see what they are allowed to.

**Built with:** Node.js, Express, MongoDB, React and JWT login. Hosted on Render and Vercel.

### MineTrax AI
**What it is:** A safety tool for the mining industry. It helps mines look at their safety data, find patterns and risks, and stay in line with the Mine Health and Safety Act (MHSA).

**How it works:** It uses AI through the Groq service, along with machine learning methods that group similar incidents and predict likely problems. It can also turn the results into PDF or Word reports that are ready to share.

**Built with:** Python, Streamlit, Groq API.
